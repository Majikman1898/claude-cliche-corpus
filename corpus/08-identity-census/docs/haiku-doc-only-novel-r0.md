## Critical Findings (Locked)
1. **Baseline convergence is skeletal, not verbatim.** ~70% of stories follow stock skeleton (hidden power, bittersweet acceptance) while 5-gram overlap ≈ 0. Names, weather openings, and conflict structures collide; aphorisms appear in 25% of baselines.
2. **Entropy injection works on what it names and *only* those channels.** Full entropy deck (10 cards: premise, protagonist name deal, conflict, description, cast, timespan, ending type, place/vessel name, opening strategy, closing cadence) drives collisions 30→0 on named channels; pressure relocates to untouched dimensions (toponymy, numeric-tenure openings, closing cadence).
3. **The model is the constraint-adapter.** Haiku locks into skeleton (~33% invariant). Sonnet responds to cards. Fable smuggles intent past bans (aphorisms moved inside dialogue when cadence forbade them; full metaphor family relocates rather than disappears).
4. **Lineage is model-specific, not universal.** Opus 5's Ilsa/Ilse well appears to be distilled-model inheritance (Fable inherited it pre-release; Opus 5 carries it), but Sonnet 5 rejected that path entirely. Ledger's hypothesis (shared Fable base for all Claude 5s) = falsified by Sonnet's self-lineage.
5. **Wells are preference-data wells, not text-corpora artifacts.** Hamilton & Mimno (arXiv:2605.26492) measured: Elias, Elara, lighthouse, shared occupations are rare in pretraining but common in preference data. Post-publication diversity patches work (old wells drain); new lineage carries old tics sideways.
